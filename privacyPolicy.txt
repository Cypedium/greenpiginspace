Got it 👍 — you want a **Privacy Policy suitable for an app rated 3+ on Google Play**. That means the policy must be **simple, transparent, and compliant with Google Play’s Families Policy Requirements**. It should reassure parents that your app is safe for children, explain what data (if any) is collected, and how it’s used.  

Here’s a **template you can adapt** for your app:

---

## 📜 Privacy Policy (for Apps Rated 3+)

**Effective Date:** [Insert Date]  
**Developer:** [Your Cypedium/Green Pig in Space]  

### 1. Introduction  
We value your privacy. This app is designed for users of all ages, including children. We follow Google Play’s Families Policy Requirements to ensure a safe and appropriate experience.

### 2. Information We Collect  
- **No personal information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers.  
- **Non-personal data:** We may collect anonymous usage statistics (such as app performance, crash reports, or basic analytics) to improve the app.  

### 3. How We Use Information  
- To maintain and improve app functionality.  
- To ensure a safe experience for children.  
- We do not use data for targeted advertising.  

### 4. Advertising & Third Parties  
- If ads are shown, they are **family-friendly** and comply with Google Play’s Families Policy.  
- We do not allow personalized ads or behavioral tracking for children.  
- Third-party SDKs used in the app are approved for child-directed services.  

### 5. Data Sharing  
We do not share personal information with third parties. Non-personal data may be shared only in aggregated form (e.g., app usage statistics).  

### 6. Security  
We use reasonable measures to protect information and keep the app safe for children.  

### 7. Parents’ Rights  
Parents can contact us at [Your Contact Email] to ask questions or request deletion of any data that may have been collected.  

### 8. Changes to This Policy  
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted within the app or on our website.
